# WELCOME TO INDRA HOTEL

Just minutes from bustling Phnom Penh, Indra Hotel offers a serene and cozy environment perfect for unwinding and relaxation. Experience the refined elegance and comfort of this contemporary sanctuary, where our dedicated team ensures top-notch service and a warm, welcoming atmosphere. 

The hotel boasts a charming cafe, fully equipped fitness center, saltwater pool, and a fine dining restaurant offering stunning city views. Our contemporary rooms are thoughtfully designed to caters to your every need and ensures optimal relaxation.

Nestled in a green and eco-friendly setting, this hidden gem exudes a sustainable charm. Shopping centers and restaurants are just a leisurely stroll or a quick 5-minute drive away. Attractions such as Wat Phnom, the Night Market, the Royal Palace, and the National Museum are all within a 15-minute drive, making Indra Hotel the perfect base for exploring Phnom Penh.
